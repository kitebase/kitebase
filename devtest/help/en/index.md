Devtest is a small library: the catalogue of the books, the members who borrow
them, the loans, and the orders sent to the publishers. It is also the bench
where every mechanism of the framework is shown working, so a few entries of
the menu are demos rather than work.

What you see depends on your role: the menu, the pages and the commands follow
it. A librarian sees the catalogue and the loans, a buyer the orders.

This manual follows the menu. Each entry has its card: what the page is for,
its commands and what its fields mean. The appendices at the end hold the
[[appendix/keyboard|keyboard shortcuts]] and a short glossary.

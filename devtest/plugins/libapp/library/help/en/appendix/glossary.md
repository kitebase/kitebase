# Glossary

**Member.** A person who may borrow books. Not a user of this application:
members have no password.

**Loan.** A book in the hands of a member, from the day it leaves to the day it
returns.

**Due date.** When a loan should end. A loan past its due date is late.

**Order.** A request to one publisher for some books, with a price and a
discount for each.

**Receipt.** What arrived from a publisher, against one of its orders.

**Working date.** The date the application proposes for new records. It is
today, unless you chose another one.

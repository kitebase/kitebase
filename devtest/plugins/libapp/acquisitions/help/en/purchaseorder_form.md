1. **Order.** Choose the [[publisher_list|publisher]] and fill in the header. If you change the
   publisher once the lines are in, the form offers to remove the books of the
   previous one, or to go back to the header.
2. **Lines.** An order needs at least one line. The reorder button of the grid lets
   you move the lines.
3. **Footer.** Add the expected delivery, and a discount on the whole order if there is one.

An error is told on the step where you can mend it. The order is written once,
when you save it on the last step.

Prepares the payment reminders in three steps. Nothing is sent until you confirm.

1. **Choose.** Set the minimum balance: customers who owe less are left out.
2. **Check.** The customers come back in a list. Open a row to change it, and tick the
   ones you want to remind.
3. **Confirm.** The reminders go out for the ticked rows, or for all of them when none
   is ticked. A summary tells how many, the total outstanding and the high-risk customers.

This is a demonstration: the customers are made up and nothing leaves the system.
